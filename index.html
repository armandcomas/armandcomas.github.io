Armand Comas Massagué

I'm a Research Scientist at Google XR, on the Syntec team, where I use multi-modal generative models to synthesize the world in 3D.

Before that, I did my PhD with Octavia Camps at Northeastern University's Robust Systems Lab, where I studied the structure underneath generation: how to interpret, manipulate, and compose representations to gain finer control over the generative process.

Along the way, I collaborated with Google XR, Adobe (Project Indigo, with Mark Levoy), Netflix, MERL, and MIT (with Yilun Du and Joshua Tenenbaum), and spent a research stay at UPenn with René Vidal.
